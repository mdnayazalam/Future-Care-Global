    📋 ATS Resume Checklist for Indian Recruiters

    1. Delhi Locality & Metro Line

    Specify your locality (e.g. Rohini, Noida Sec 62) and nearby Metro line so recruiters instantly recognize commute feasibility.

    2. Transparent Notice Period

    State exact notice (e.g., Immediate, 15 days, 30 days). Candidates with clear notice get 3x higher shortlisting priority.

    3. Quantified Achievements

    Include INR revenue generated, team size led, or % process improvement rather than passive bulleted job duties.

    4. Single-Column ATS Layout

    Avoid complex tables and multi-column Canva templates that get garbled by Indian enterprise recruitment systems.

    🎯 3-Step Interview Preparation Blueprint

    1. The STAR Framework: Structure behavioural answers around Situation, Task, Action, and Measurable Result.
    2. Delhi/NCR Commute Confidence: When asked about office presence in Noida or Gurgaon, provide crisp confirmation regarding Metro lines and travel logistics.
    3. Salary Negotiation Etiquette: Always anchor discussions around total Fixed CTC vs. Performance Variable bonus rather than vague percentage jumps.
