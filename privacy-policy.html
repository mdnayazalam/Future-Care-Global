1. Introduction & Scope

Future Care Global ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a premier recruitment and manpower consultancy firm headquartered at Azadpur, North Delhi, Delhi 110033. Established in 2016, we connect aspiring candidates with verified corporate employers across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, and pan-India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect information when you visit our website (futurecareglobal.com), submit your curriculum vitae (CV) or resume, respond to job vacancies, or communicate with our recruitment consultants via phone, email, or WhatsApp.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to assess your qualifications for employment or to fulfill employer recruitment mandates:

  • Candidate Identifiers: Full legal name, email address, mobile/WhatsApp contact number, date of birth, and current residential locality in Delhi/NCR or home state.
  • Professional & Educational Profile: Resumes/CVs (.pdf, .docx), academic degrees, past employment history, job titles, current and expected CTC/salary, notice period duration, and technical competencies.
  • Communication Records: WhatsApp correspondence with our authorized recruitment desks, email exchanges, and notes from preliminary screening interviews.
  • Employer Information: Corporate organization names, registered office addresses, GST/PAN credentials, hiring manager contact details, and job descriptions (JDs).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your submitted information is used strictly for legitimate talent acquisition and staffing operations:

  • Evaluating candidate suitability for specific full-time, contract, or executive job openings.
  • Transmitting shortlisted profiles to corporate hiring managers with candidate consent.
  • Coordinating interview schedules, skill assessments, and offer rollouts.
  • Verifying professional credentials, employment references, and background validation where mandated by clients.
  • Providing career guidance, salary benchmarking, and timely status updates regarding candidate applications.

4. 100% Free Candidate Policy & Anti-Fraud Guarantee

🛡️ Strict Zero-Fee Protection for Jobseekers

Future Care Global maintains an uncompromising 100% Free Placement Policy for candidates. We NEVER charge any registration fees, interview booking fees, processing charges, or security deposits from job applicants at any stage of the recruitment process.

Our consultancy fees are paid exclusively by our verified corporate client employers upon successful candidate onboarding. If anyone claiming to represent Future Care Global requests money, bank transfers, or UPI payments from you, please report them immediately to our official grievance desk at hello@futurecareglobal.com or WhatsApp +91 8826140781.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We value the trust you place in us. We never sell, rent, or trade candidate personal information to third-party telemarketers, credit agencies, or advertising brokers.

  • Corporate Client Employers: Candidate CVs and profiles are shared solely with registered hiring companies where an active candidate-job alignment has been identified.
  • Statutory Authorities: We may disclose data if legally required by Indian regulatory or law enforcement bodies pursuant to applicable laws or court orders.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect all resumes, contact databases, and candidate submissions from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All file uploads are stored in restricted-access directories, and access to candidate repositories is restricted exclusively to authorized recruitment officers under strict non-disclosure obligations.

7. Candidate Data Rights & Correction

You retain full authority over your personal information:

  • Right to Update: You may submit an updated version of your resume or correct your phone number/email at any time.
  • Right to Withdraw: You may request the deactivation or complete deletion of your resume from our active candidate database by emailing hello@futurecareglobal.com.
  • Communication Opt-Out: You may opt out of WhatsApp job alerts at any time by replying "STOP" to our recruitment desk.

8. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses basic session cookies and aggregated analytics strictly to improve site responsiveness, analyze traffic patterns across Delhi/NCR localities, and ensure form submission reliability. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking or behavioral profiling scripts.
